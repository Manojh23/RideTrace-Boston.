# Short narrated app demonstration — recording guide (about 90 seconds)

This is a **script to record after deploying and seeding the actual app**. It is not an already completed public video.

1. **00:00–00:12** — Show the overview: “This is RideTrace Boston, our MBTA commuting reliability and rider transfer-experience web application. We built it with Vanilla JavaScript, Node, Express and MongoDB.”
2. **00:12–00:28** — Open My commutes; submit a sample journey. “This is Manoj's independent full-stack collection. The record is saved to MongoDB and is private to this browser.”
3. **00:28–00:44** — Edit, delete and filter. Point to charts and buffer. “The 80th percentile of positive historical delays provides the suggested departure buffer after at least three entries.”
4. **00:44–01:02** — Open Transfer board and submit a transfer report. “This is Kavya's independent CRUD collection. Reports are public, unverified, and include route-to-route transfer times and missed connections.”
5. **01:02–01:17** — Filter by stop/daypart; show median and missed percentage. Edit and remove your own report.
6. **01:17–01:30** — Show the seeded MBTA stop autocomplete, browser identity limitation, and app links. “Both features use a shared read-only catalog, but neither accesses the other's editable collection.”
