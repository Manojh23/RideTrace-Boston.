QUICK START GUIDE

Everything you need to ride with context.

RideTrace is a class project designed to complement official transit information with personal and community-reported history.

01

Log a journey

Open My commutes, enter the route label, date, expected minutes, actual minutes and reason. Save, edit, or delete your own logs. Filter the history by route or weekday.

02

Understand the buffer

With at least three commute logs, your dashboard shows the rounded-up 80th percentile of positive delays as a suggested departure buffer. The chart shows averages by weekday. These figures are historical, not forecasts.

03

Explore a connection

Open the Transfer board and filter by stop, from/to routes, or time of day. Read user reports, typical transfer duration, and missed-connection frequency.

04

Share your experience

Submit a dated transfer report with actual minutes and optional notes. Your report appears publicly as unverified. From the same browser, you can edit or delete it.

Privacy and data

Your commute logs are associated with a random, signed, HttpOnly browser cookie and are accessible only from that browser identity. No user account or personal email is collected. If you clear your browser cookies, you may lose access to older entries; the records do not automatically disappear. Transfer reports are public, so do not enter identifying or sensitive information.

The stop catalog is imported from the MBTA GTFS feed and credited to MBTA / MassDOT. The catalog is read-only. App reports are not official MBTA reports, and there are no live feeds, predictions, official alerts or verified transfer promises.

For current transit status, accessibility alerts, emergencies, and journey planning, consult the MBTA website.
